# Evidence publication

This directory contains reports, result measurements, logs, configuration, protocols and validation receipts. Experiment scripts, implementation patches and source manifests remain in the [immutable RemoteFiles archive](https://github.com/cuzmi/RemoteFiles/tree/fcdbdcd2d00efb20b77549574c19b0f11f607abf/research/minicpm_given_text_stream_h100_20261027). Audio and full source bundles were not published.

Original copied files are byte-identical to the archive commit. PROVENANCE.json records their hashes and URLs. Root SHA256SUMS.json covers all published files except itself. Publication did not rerun experiments; prepared protocols are not completed validation.
